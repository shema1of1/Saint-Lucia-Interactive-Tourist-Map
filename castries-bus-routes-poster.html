ROUTE ZONES

Zone 1 · North — Gros Islet, Rodney Bay & Pigeon Island corridor. Route 1A runs most frequently, roughly every 5 minutes.
Zone 2 · South & East — Vieux Fort (2H) and Dennery (2B), plus local variants 2A & 2C.
Zone 3 · West — Soufrière corridor (3D, 3F) and connecting west-coast variants 3A, 3B, 3C, 3E.
Zone 5 · Local — Shorter feeder and in-town routes serving areas close to Castries.

HOW TO RIDE

1
Spot the bus. Licensed minibuses have a green plate starting with "M."
2
Flag it down. Raise your hand at any stop along the route.
3
Open your own door. Doors are manual — open and close them yourself.
4
Pay the driver. Cash only, through the front passenger window.
5
Call your stop. Say "Bus stop!" loudly as your stop approaches.
6
No fixed schedule. Buses depart once full — most run Mon–Sat.
